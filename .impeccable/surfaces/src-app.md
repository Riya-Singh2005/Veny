## Scope
The Veny app shell and its key screens: the customer home, service detail and vendor dashboard, plus the shared design system they use. Mode: **Operate**. The marketing site is separate (later) and has its own references.

## Audience and job
Urban metro households book a verified pro into a real free slot, mostly on a phone. Vendors run their day (accept, start, complete) from the dashboard. Proof is real verification status, availability and reviews only. Never fabricate claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The Elite Marketplace as a Bombay Art Deco foyer at night: calm, geometric, gilded detail on a working product. It refuses the category default of white rounded photo cards with an icon row, and the old app's neon cosmic glow.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Colour:** ink-violet lacquer ground (#0E0A18) with one-step-raised panels; brass hairline seams (no shadows); amethyst for primary actions only; ivory text; sea-green for success.
- **Type:** Bodoni Moda (Didone display) for headings, names and prices; Manrope for UI with tabular numerals.
- **Corners:** small radii (8–14px). The stepped Deco corner is reserved for the signature.

STORY: The visitor sees that every pro is verified and that time slots are real, then finds a pro, picks a lit slot and books in a few taps. A vendor sees their day as time-ranked rows.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- **Header:** wordmark plus stepped mark, nav, location and auth, under a brass hairline.
- **Left 7 of 12 columns:** a 76px Didone headline, the lede, then search (service, when, primary button).
- **Right 5 columns:** a stepped brass frame holding the next free pro with slot chips and a Book button.
- **Bottom:** a hairline-ruled band of the 6 categories.

Signature: the stepped Deco frame around the slot picker and the verified seal. Raises:
- one importance ramp (size and weight);
- hairline seams, with destructive actions isolated (from Dark Console);
- the photo owns the first screen of mobile detail (from Vertical Feed);
- the vendor day as time-ranked rows restyled in place (from Split-Flap).

FORM: Bombay Deco Nights, position 6 on the grounded list; seed: e1d298a53ed7d665.
